About Ajwa & origin

Ajwa dates from Madinah: a one-of-a-kind variety

Ajwa is the signature date of Madinah (Al-Madinah Al-Munawwarah), Saudi Arabia. Small to medium in size and very dark brown to almost black, it has soft flesh and a gentle, mild sweetness compared with many other varieties.

Highly sought after for its cultural and spiritual value, especially during Ramadan, Hajj and Umrah, authentic Ajwa is grown almost exclusively in the Madinah region. That is why origin and quality selection matter so much to wholesale buyers.

Seasonality

Ajwa harvest season

In Madinah, Ajwa is typically harvested from late summer into early autumn: the season starts around late July, the main harvest generally takes place in August and continues into September. Thanks to cold storage, wholesale Ajwa dates are available all year round.

Harvest window

Generally late July to September in Madinah, depending on the year. The fruit is picked at the tamr stage (fully ripe and dry).

Peak quality

The heart of the season, usually August, brings the widest choice of fresh-crop fruit: the best time to reserve new-crop volumes.

Year-round availability

Properly packed and cold-stored, Ajwa keeps its texture and colour, so supply stays steady outside the harvest window.

Plan ahead for demand peaks

Demand rises sharply before Ramadan, which moves earlier every year, and around Hajj and Umrah. For those periods, plan your wholesale orders well in advance.

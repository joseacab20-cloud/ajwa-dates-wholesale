Privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This page explains what data is collected when you visit the Ajwa Dates Wholesale website (https://ajwadateswholesale.vercel.app/), why, how long it is kept and how to exercise your rights.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

The data controller is the publisher of the Ajwa Dates Wholesale website. For any question about your data, contact us on WhatsApp at +212 664 738 219.

2. What data is collected?

This site has no forms, no customer accounts and no online payments. The only data collected on the site comes from Google Analytics 4 audience measurement and Google Ads advertising measurement, used to measure visits and WhatsApp contacts. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for their consent first:

  • the pages you view, the date and length of your visit, and the page you came from (search engine, social network, direct link);
  • technical information: device type, operating system, browser, language, screen resolution;
  • an approximate location (country, region, city), derived from your IP address. According to Google, Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses;
  • clicks on the WhatsApp buttons (where the button is on the page and the link address);
  • a random identifier stored in a cookie, which tells visits and visitors apart without knowing who you are;
  • if you reach the site by clicking one of our Google ads, the identifier of that ad click ("gclid"), which shows whether the visit from the ad led to a click on a WhatsApp button (a "conversion").

This data is used for visit statistics, to understand how the site is used and improve it, and to measure the performance of our ads (how many visits from an ad lead to a WhatsApp enquiry). In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the legal basis is your consent; elsewhere, it is our legitimate interest in measuring and improving the site and our ads, which you can object to at any time (see section 4).

Google Ads may also use your visits to this site to show you our ads on other sites (remarketing and personalised advertising). Our Google Analytics property is linked to our Google Ads account for these purposes. You can turn this off at any time (see section 4).

3. Cookies and local storage

The Google tag (Google Analytics and Google Ads) is loaded on every page. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a banner asks for your consent and, until you click "Accept" (or if you click "Decline"), Google's Consent Mode is set to "denied": no Google Analytics or Google Ads cookie is set, no WhatsApp click is counted as an ad conversion, and Google only receives cookieless, aggregate signals (for example that a page was viewed) without an identifier that would recognise you. If you arrive from an ad, the ad click identifier may stay in the address (URL) of the pages you then view, without being stored in a cookie. To tell whether to show the banner, the site checks your country from your IP address (through our host, Vercel); this country is not stored.

4. Your consent and how to opt out

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a banner asks you on your first visit to accept or decline audience measurement (Google Analytics) and advertising cookies (Google Ads). Declining is as easy as accepting and does not stop you from using the site. Your choice is kept for 6 months, after which the banner appears again. You can change your mind at any time: (also available through the "Manage cookies" link at the bottom of every page). If you decline or withdraw your consent, the Google Analytics cookies (_ga…) and Google Ads cookies (_gcl…) are deleted.

Wherever you are, you can also opt out:

  • in your browser settings, by blocking or deleting cookies for this site (or third-party cookies);
  • in your Google account, by turning off personalised ads in My Ad Center (Google ad settings);
  • for Google Analytics, by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

5. How long data is kept

  • Google Analytics statistics: 14 months maximum, then deleted automatically.
  • Google Analytics cookies: 13 months maximum.
  • Google Ads cookies: 90 days.
  • Your consent choice (EEA, UK and Switzerland): 6 months.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp: as long as needed to answer your request and follow up on your order.

6. Who receives this data?

  • Google (Google Analytics), as a service provider for audience measurement, and Google (Google Ads), for measuring the performance of our ads and, where allowed (in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, with your consent), remarketing and personalised advertising. For Google Ads, Google also uses this data under its own rules: how Google uses data from sites that use its services. Data may be transferred to the United States; Google LLC is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Vercel, the site's host, which processes technical logs (including IP addresses) needed to run and secure the site.
  • WhatsApp: when you click a WhatsApp button, you leave the site; the messages you send are handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy.

We never sell your personal data.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Union, the United Kingdom or certain US states), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To exercise these rights, message us on WhatsApp (+212 664 738 219). Because Google Analytics and Google Ads data is pseudonymous, we cannot always link it to a specific person; the simplest option is then to decline or withdraw your consent, or use the opt-out options in section 4, and delete your cookies.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can file a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated, for example if new tools are added to the site. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.
